01 / OUR PURPOSE

Access.
Visibility.
Connection.

South Asian leadership should not be the exception. We are building the relationships and infrastructure to make it a norm.

SALUTE is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and curated private professional network. We bring South Asian women and allies together across industries and generations to build meaningful relationships, share expertise, and open doors for one another.

Access, curation, and connection are the principles SALUTE is built on. Members and allies share expertise, make introductions, and champion one another. These relationships continue beyond any one gathering.
